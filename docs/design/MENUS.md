# Menus & Overlay Design: VORIXA

Menus are non-modal overlays that retain background world ambience:
* **Pause Menu**: Freezes simulation clock, blurs 3D viewport with radial shader filter, presents Resume, Controls, Audio, Graphics, and Abort Expedition.
* **Codex Screen**: Interactive 3D holographic wireframe viewer allowing inspection of discovered planetary relics and enemy automaton weaknesses.
