# Accessibility (a11y) Standards: VORIXA

VORIXA treats accessibility as a foundational engineering constraint, not a post-launch patch.

## Standards & Capabilities
1. **Full Input Remapping**: Every action on keyboard, mouse, and gamepad can be completely rebound.
2. **Colorblind Palettes**: Selectable HUD color themes for Protanopia, Deuteranopia, and Tritanopia.
3. **Audio Subtitles & Visual Cues**: Directional damage and audio indicators displayed on the visor reticle.
4. **Motion Sickness Accommodations**: Independent toggles for Camera Bobbing, Motion Blur, FOV adjustment, and Crosshair Dot stabilizer.
