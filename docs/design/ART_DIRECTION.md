# Art Direction: VORIXA

The aesthetic identity of VORIXA is **Futuristic Brutalism meets Bioluminescent Wonder**:
* **Scale**: Colossal monolithic structures and jagged geometric crystalline spires contrasting against deep atmospheric canyons.
* **Lighting**: Deep void shadows pierced by vibrant, high-luminance emission accents (plasma conduits, energy shields, alien flora).
* **Palette**: Dark slate backgrounds (`#0a0c10`) accented with radiant cyan (`#00f0ff`), neon amber (`#ffb703`), and ion purple (`#7209b7`).
