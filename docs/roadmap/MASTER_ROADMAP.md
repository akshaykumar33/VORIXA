# Master Phased Roadmap: VORIXA

The development of VORIXA is structured into 9 disciplined, milestone-driven phases. We do not advance to subsequent phases until the exit criteria and automated quality gates of the preceding phase are validated.

## Master Roadmap Overview

```text
[Phase 00: Foundation] ────> [Phase 01: Vertical Slice] ────> [Phase 02: Gameplay Mechanics]
        │                                                              │
        ▼                                                              ▼
[Phase 03: Procedural World] ─> [Phase 04: AI & Ecosystems] ──> [Phase 05: Progression & RPG]
        │                                                              │
        ▼                                                              ▼
[Phase 06: Multiplayer] ──────> [Phase 07: Natural Input & XR] ─> [Phase 08: Production Hardening]
```
