# Phase 02: Gameplay Mechanics & Combat

* **Status**: Planned
* **Prerequisites**: Phase 01 Vertical Slice

## Objectives
Implement the fluid kinetic momentum toolkit (slides, air dashes, wall-runs) and plasma weaponry.

## Key Deliverables
1. **Kinetic Movement Suite**:
   - Momentum preservation slide on decline slopes.
   - Omnidirectional horizontal air dash with cooldown recharge.
   - Wall-running against vertical surfaces with 45-degree dismount impulse.
2. **Arc Carbine Weapon System**:
   - Projectile raycasting with muzzle flare and impact decals.
   - Heat/stamina resource consumption and passive dissipation.
   - Hit feedback markers and spatial audio impact cues.
