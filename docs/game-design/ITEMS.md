# Items, Economy & Dynamic Difficulty: VORIXA

## 1. Item Architecture

Items fall into 3 operational categories:
1. **Raw Resources**: Refined Silica, Hyper-dense Plasma, Monolith Resonance Fragments (used in crafting).
2. **Suit Modules**: Passive mod chips (e.g., *Kinetic Dampener*, *Overcharge Heat Sink*).
3. **Consumables**: Nanite Repair Injectors, Emergency Jump Thruster Canisters, Portable EMP Beacons.

---

## 2. In-Game Economy
* **Zero Pay-to-Win**: All economy loops are purely driven by in-game exploration and tactical skill.
* **Scarcity & Risk**: Higher risk biomes yield exponentially higher concentrations of Monolith Fragments.
* **Refinement Conversion**: Players refine raw mineral ores at base camp into permanent engineering alloy.

---

## 3. Dynamic Difficulty Scaling (The "Entropy Index")
As the player remains in an expedition world, the planetary **Entropy Index** increments every 3 minutes:
* Enemy spawn frequency increases by 8%.
* Elite variants acquire secondary elemental mutations.
* Weather events (radiation squalls, magnetic storms) become more frequent.
* Forces a tension curve: linger to collect more resonance crystals, or evacuate before being overwhelmed.
