# Combat System: VORIXA

Combat in VORIXA emphasizes projectile awareness, hit precision, mobility integration, and dynamic elemental status effects.

## 1. Weapon Classes
* **Arc Carbine**: High-velocity linear plasma repeater. Baseline scout rifle with negligible drop.
* **Shard Scatterer**: Close-range micro-crystal shotgun firing tight clusters with high physical stagger.
* **Resonance Rail**: Charged sniper weapon penetrating multiple enemies and crystal formations.
* **Vortex Launcher**: Slow-traveling gravity sphere that pulls lightweight mobs into a singularity before detonating.

## 2. Damage Types & Elemental Reactions

| Damage Type | Visual Indicator | Status Effect | Synergistic Reaction |
| ----------- | ---------------- | ------------- | -------------------- |
| **Thermal** | Amber / Orange Glow | *Ignite*: Damage over time | When applied to Cryo target: *Thermal Shock* (instant 250% burst damage) |
| **Cryo** | Cyan / Ice Spikes | *Chill*: Movement slowed by 40%; stacks to *Frozen* | When shattered with Kinetic: AoE frost shrapnel |
| **Kinetic** | White / Impact Shock | *Stagger*: Interrupts enemy charge attacks | Knocks flying drones into terrain geometry for bonus impact damage |
| **Disruption**| Purple / Electric Arcs | *EMP*: Strips energy shields; disables automaton sensors | Overloaded shields detonate into an electrical chain arc |
