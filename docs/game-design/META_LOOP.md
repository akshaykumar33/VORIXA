# Meta Loop Architecture: VORIXA

The overarching progression architecture connects individual procedural expeditions into a persistent galactic journey.

## 1. Waypoint Archive & Expedition Cycle
Between planetary descents, the player manages their Voidstrider profile from the **Waypoint Archive**:
* **Star Map Navigation**: Selecting planetary seeds with varied danger indices, resource yields, and atmospheric modifiers.
* **Tech Synthesis**: Combining scanned blueprints with harvested isotopes to fabricate permanent suit upgrades.
* **Expedition Mutators**: Applying voluntary difficulty handicaps (e.g., *Atmospheric Corrosion*, *Night Cycle Only*) to multiply rare drop rates.

## 2. Retention Loops
* **Discovery Codex**: Cataloging unique procedural flora, fauna species, and anomaly types.
* **Sector Mastery**: Reaching the core monolith in all 4 primary biome types unlocks the *Void Continuum* endless expedition mode.
