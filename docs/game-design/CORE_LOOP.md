# Core Loop & Meta Loop: VORIXA

## Core Loop (Minute-to-Minute)

```text
[Scan Horizon for Relic / Beacon]
             ↓
[Execute Kinetic Traversal (Run/Slide/Dash)]
             ↓
[Discover Anomalous Site]
             ↓
[Engage Hostile Automated Defenses]
             ↓
[Harvest Resonance Cores & Data Slates]
             ↓
[Refill Oxygen / Recharge Shield Battery]
```

* **Target Cadence**: 45–90 seconds per micro-engagement.
* **Momentum Continuity**: Encountering enemies does not force player to stop; combat is interwoven with high-speed movement.

---

## Meta Loop (Session-to-Session)

```text
[Expedition Deployment]
             ↓
[Collect Relics, Mineral Isotopes, Tech Blueprints]
             ↓
[Reach Biome Monolith / Overload Beacon]
             ↓
[Survive Extraction Apex Event]
             ↓
[Return to Orbital Waypoint Archive]
             ↓
[Upgrade Persistent Suit Traits & Unlock Weapon Blueprints]
```

* **Session Length**: 15 to 30 minutes per expedition loop.
* **Loss Penalty**: Death retains 50% of raw scanned data slates, but drops physical unrefined isotopes at point of death (recoverable).
