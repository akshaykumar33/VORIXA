# Progression & RPG Systems: VORIXA

Progression balances session-based roguelite run empowerment with long-term account mastery.

## 1. Voidstrider Suit Specializations
Players allocate resonance nodes into three distinct branches:

```text
               +----------------------------------+
               |        RESONANCE MATRIX          |
               +----------------------------------+
                /               |                \
               v                v                 v
     [Vanguard (Kinetic)]  [Surveyor (Traversal)] [Siphon (Energy)]
     + Max Health          + Slide Friction Loss  + Shield Recharge Rate
     + Armor Thickness     + Air-Dash Charges     + Overcharge Duration
     + Weapon Stability    + Thruster Efficiency  + Elemental Synergy
```

## 2. In-Run Augments
Defeating elite guardians yields immediate temporary augments:
* **Overclocked Capacitor**: Eliminates air-dash cooldown for 6 seconds following an enemy defeat.
* **Reflective Plating**: Parrying within 0.1s reflects hostile energy projectiles back at the attacker.
* **Kinetic Dynamo**: Sliding continuously builds up next shot damage multiplier (up to 300%).
