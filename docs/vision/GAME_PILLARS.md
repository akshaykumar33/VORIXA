# Game Pillars: VORIXA

The four foundational pillars guide every design, feature, and architectural decision:

```text
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  VORIXA                                       |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------------+
| 1. Boundless Horizon  | 2. Kinetic Precision  | 3. Emergent Ecosystems        |
|    Infinite procedural|    Sub-frame responsive|    Living world simulation    |
|    worlds & streaming |    momentum & combat  |    reactive AI & weather      |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------------+
|                       4. Web-Native Sovereignty                               |
|                          Zero friction, zero installs, multiplatform reach    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## Pillar 1: Boundless Horizon (Procedural Infinity)
- Players explore seamless planetary surfaces without artificial loading screens.
- Multi-scale procedural noise (fractal Brownian motion, Voronoi cellular automata, domain warping) drives geography, cave systems, flora, and mineral outcroppings.
- Everything is streamed asynchronously within an active simulation radius around the player.

## Pillar 2: Kinetic Precision (Fluid Movement & Combat)
- Movement is rewarding in itself: momentum preservation, sliding, wall-running, directional dashes, and vertical mantle mechanics.
- Combat prioritizes deliberate timing, animation cancels, active dodging, and distinct weapon handling profiles rather than pure stat checks.

## Pillar 3: Emergent Ecosystems (Living Worlds)
- Entities operate within an environmental matrix: temperature, atmospheric density, day/night cycles, and dynamic weather fronts.
- Fauna exhibit realistic behaviors: territorial defense, herd migration, nocturnal hunting, and reactivity to player weaponry.

## Pillar 4: Web-Native Sovereignty (Frictionless Distribution)
- Native web standards (WebGPU, WebAssembly, Web Audio API, Web Workers, WebRTC, WebXR).
- No proprietary launchers, app store approvals, or multi-gigabyte initial patch downloads.
